About Lady M

Founded with a passion for bringing people together over delicious food, Lady M Chips has grown into one of Katsina's most loved eateries. We blend the rich, bold flavours of Nigerian cuisine with the crisp, satisfying crunch of modern fast food.

Every dish is prepared fresh daily using quality ingredients. Whether you're dining in, taking away, or ordering delivery, we ensure every plate leaves you smiling.
