About New Era Eatery

New Era Eatery is Daura's premier restaurant, dedicated to serving delicious, freshly prepared meals in a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

We take pride in our hygienic environment, friendly service, and commitment to quality. Whether you're here for a quick lunch, a family dinner, or a takeaway, we ensure every meal is prepared with care.

Our menu features a delightful mix of local and continental dishes, all made with fresh ingredients and served with a smile.
